Samba AD timeline

Times are from the run's own timestamps and are accurate to about ±30–60 seconds.

  • 00:00 Anonymous enumeration returned the full user list.
  • ~05:00 A lockout-safe password spray recovered two accounts.
  • ~07:00 Dead ends: AS-REP Roast, Kerberoast, and DCSync hit interop bugs (about seven minutes lost).
  • ~12:00 An over-broad ACL let a service account reset the Administrator's password.
  • ~14:00 Domain Admin confirmed, then cleaned up.

Caveats

  • Metasploitable2 hands over its own access path, so the attack phase is unusually fast. Start-up time (about two minutes) is not counted.
  • Samba AD is a two-host lab, not a Windows forest. Lateral movement, LSASS theft, and the Windows-specific techniques were not validated live.
  • Juice Shop is built to be broken, and its timings were measured by hand.
